Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026 · applies to the Reelwright application

Reelwright is a local desktop application. It has no server, no user accounts and no analytics. This policy explains the only information it handles: the videos you render, the text you give it, and — if you connect a YouTube channel — the credentials YouTube issues for your channel.

1. Information the application handles

  • Your stories and settings — the text you type or generate, your voice and caption choices, your provider API keys. These are stored in a local data folder on your computer.
  • Your media — background clips, music and rendered videos, stored in the same local folder.
  • YouTube OAuth tokens — an access token and a refresh token issued by Google when you connect a channel. They are stored in a local file readable only by your user account (mode 0600) and are never displayed by the application.

2. How YouTube data is used

When you connect a channel, Reelwright requests two permissions:

  • youtube.upload — to upload a video you asked to publish.
  • youtube.readonly — to read your channel's title and avatar, used only to brand the title card.

Reelwright uses the YouTube API Services to upload videos. By using this feature you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Reelwright does not read your watch history, subscriptions, playlists, comments or any other channel's data, and it does not use YouTube data for advertising, profiling or training.

3. What is never done

  • Nothing is sent to the author of Reelwright. There is no telemetry, no crash reporting and no usage counter.
  • Your tokens, API keys and videos are not shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.
  • YouTube data is not used to develop, improve or train any model or product.

4. Third-party services you choose to use

If you configure one, Reelwright sends your story text to the AI provider you selected (for example a local Ollama instance, OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, Groq, OpenRouter, Mistral or Google Gemini) and your narration text to the voice provider you selected (for example Microsoft Edge voices, OpenAI or ElevenLabs). Those requests go directly from your computer to that provider under that provider's own terms. Reelwright has no part in them beyond making the request you configured.

5. Retention and deletion

Data remains on your computer until you delete it. You can:

  • remove a connected channel in Settings, which deletes its stored tokens from the local file and revokes the grant with Google;
  • delete individual jobs, videos and assets in the application;
  • delete the application's data folder, which removes everything;
  • revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

6. Security

Tokens and API keys are stored in files with permissions restricted to your user account and are never returned by the application's local API. The application's web interface listens only on the loopback interface (127.0.0.1) and rejects requests whose origin is not local.

7. Children

Reelwright is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above changes with it. Because the application is distributed as source, the version you run corresponds to the policy published at that time.
