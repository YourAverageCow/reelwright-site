Reelwright — user flow

What the single user does, in order. Every step is visible in the UI and every failure names its cause.

1

Write or generate

Type a premise, or press Generate story. The model streams into an editable text box.

provider: local Ollama by default

→
2

Pick a look and a voice

Five caption presets, four bundled fonts, voice preview. The 9:16 frame shows the real caption at real scale, with the renderer's own line breaks.

→
3

Add a background

Upload a clip or image into the library, or render on a gradient. Optional music, ducked under the narration.

→
4

Render

One press. Stage + percentage appear live; the job survives a restart; cancel kills ffmpeg.

~15 s for a 90 s video on 12 cores

→
5

Publish to YouTube

Optional. Sign in with Google once (loopback redirect, PKCE). Then Publish on a finished job: the app uploads it to the channel that authorized, and shows the watch URL.

only videos.insert is ever called

Consent

Exactly one scope: youtube.upload. The consent screen names Reelwright and links its privacy policy and terms; access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Uploads are explicit

There is no scheduler and no background publishing. A job is uploaded only when the user presses Publish on that job, or when a batch he started finishes and he chooses to publish it.

What is read from YouTube

Nothing beyond the upload response. No channel listing, no analytics, no other users' data.
