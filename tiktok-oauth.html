Reelwright — TikTok authorization

This page is the web fallback for Reelwright's TikTok login. The desktop application does not use it: it receives its authorization on a loopback address (http://127.0.0.1:8877/api/tiktok/oauth/callback) that never leaves your computer.

If you arrived here from TikTok, you can close this tab and return to Reelwright.
